The maker behind the magic

Born from
poetry &
patience.

Doriyana began as Khushi Verma's quiet rebellion — a way to turn thread into wearable art, to merge the slow beauty of traditional crochet with the visual language of couture.

Each accessory is handmade from start to finish, often inspired by a poem, a season, or the way light falls on a lazy afternoon. No two pieces are identical.
